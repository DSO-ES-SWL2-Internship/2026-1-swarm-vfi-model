The loss rate is not the probability. The probability is the chance that a burst starts at a frame. A burst then removes 1 to “longest burst” frames, and the frame after it is always kept. With 0.3 and 3, about 38% of the frames are lost, not 30%. The first frame is never lost.

2. How a gap is filled

■ kept frame○ where the frame should be◆ what the model actually makes

Each missing frame is made by the model from two existing frames, as their midpoint, so a gap of n missing frames costs exactly n model calls. Halving is exact only when the distance between the two kept frames (missing + 1) is a power of two: 1 missing frame (distance 2) or 3 missing (distance 4).
